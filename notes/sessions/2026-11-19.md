## Intra-oceanic arcs (current plan step 1; user: "continue")

Measured (scratch worktree `agent-scratch/arc-diag/`): arc-like crust stays thin (~90% < 12 km),
its felsic mass mostly subducts again (1.9–2.3 of 3.1–3.3 Gt/yr), little matures. Plan
`plans/intra-oceanic-arcs.md` (concentrate the arc profile). Review: not sound as written — the
profile alone does not thicken arcs; ~43% of the subducted arc crust goes down at its own active
boundary: below arriving buoyant crust (the per-cell rule keeps it on top) and on reversed
neighbouring segments. Implemented: overridden upper-plate arc crust (not normal oceanic crust) is
accreted onto the arriving buoyant crust; arc additions skip cells bordering the partner along a
segment where the partner overrides; `arc_profile` [0, 1, 0.5], `accretion_profile` [1, 0.5].
Tests: arc crust grows behind the trench; wedged margin vs oceanic / arc / continental upper plate
(the arc case fails without the fix). A first version that also accreted normal oceanic upper-
plate crust stacked 4–7 Gt/yr of ocean floor onto continents (collision length 14–22k km) and was
restricted. Result: felsic + sediment 4-seed mean −0.97 Gt/yr after 150 Myr (target met; was
−1.56); collision length 12–17k km; sediment part of the sea level +58 m per 100 Myr at
400–500 Myr (4-seed mean, worse than +25 before; large seed spread).

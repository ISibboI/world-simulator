Scratch diagnostic (seeds 0–3 × 1000 Myr, `agent-scratch/arc-diag/cs-log-*`, CONTSED lines):
sediment on continental-origin crust at 1000 Myr 1.08–1.24e9 Gt (within Earth's platform/orogen

the shelves as flat coastal land, which hardly erodes. Test `--set erosion.prograde=1` (8 seeds ×
1000 Myr, `agent-scratch/prograde/`): overfill 1.51 → 1.05 Gt/yr, but continental growth 0.85 →

Margins within reach are full even to shelf depth: too little accommodation. On Earth stretched
margins subside several km over ~100 Myr by thermal subsidence; in the model stretching covers
< half of the continental opening and COB crust stays ~26 km (issue 1r/7). Proposed next: margin
stretching and subsidence (current plan item 3).

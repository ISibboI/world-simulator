### Issue 3 step 2: buoyant crust resists subduction

`Crust::resists_subduction()` (crustal buoyancy ≥ 2000 m: arc/continental crust ≳ 13–15 km,
basaltic plateaus ≳ 17 km; Cloos 1993) replaces "continental" wherever subductability is
decided: overlap consumption (buoyant losers are stacked onto the overriding plate, any
winner), segment arrivals/flips and new-segment polarity (`polarity.rs`), and the collision /
trench boundary kinds (`dynamics.rs`). Ledger term "subducted continental" removed (cannot
happen). Test: an arc arriving at a trench is never subducted (stays on top against an oceanic
upper plate, stacked against a continent). Seeds 0–3, 500 Myr: substeps felsic +6…+9 Gt/yr
(was +1…+6), continental mass at 500 Myr 1.16–1.25e10 Gt; mean land 0.98–1.31 km; sediment
subduction 11–22 Gt/yr [Earth 3–4] is now the main sink; subducted transitional crust 3.8–5.

### Issue 3 step 4: trench-fill accretion (done before step 3, as the main lever)

`frontal_accretion` (flat 20%) replaced by `subduction_channel_thickness` (1 km): sediment on a
subducting sample beyond 1 km is scraped off into the accretionary wedge (metasediment), the
rest is subducted (von Huene & Scholl 1991; Clift & Vannucchi 2004). Seeds 0–3, 500 Myr: net
sediment subduction 2.4–3.1 Gt/yr [Earth 2.6–4.5], accretion 9–14 Gt/yr; felsic + sediment
balanced in the last interval (−0.6…+2.8 Gt/yr; interval rates −5…+3.5). The "continental"
class still falls 1.80 → 1.44–1.49e10 Gt (material moves into the sediment stock and
metasediment wedges); mean land rises again to 1.2–1.5 km by 500 Myr (accreted wedges), mean
ocean age 58–85 Myr, production 104–146 Gt/yr.

neighbouring cells as a thickness diffusion (`collapse_diffusivity` 10⁴ km²/Myr; channel flow,

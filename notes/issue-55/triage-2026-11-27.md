## Caveats

- The runs predate #217 (steady-state start, 21 plates by default). The steady-state metrics
  above (temperatures, plate size after 500 Myr) should not depend much on the start.
- The #155 slope comes from variation over time within a seed (geography and CO₂ change too),
  not from a controlled warming experiment; both presets give 6–8 %/K, consistent with the
  original 7 %/K.
- "Tropical lowland" is land below 500 m without lakes, within 20° of the equator, compared per
  5° band with the sea cells of the band (cells are of equal area).

## Findings, by importance

1. **The +7 K highland excess is the lowland's cold bias, not plateau physics.** The model's tropical lowland (< 300 m, |lat| < 25°) is 4.6–5.3 K colder than the ocean in its own 5° band, in main and in the branch alike (500–1000 Myr; −6.6 K at t = 0 in seed 0). The branch pins land above 3 km to the band's sea-surface temperature: reduced to sea level it is band ocean +0.5 K (−0.8…+1.4 across seeds). The same highland is +7.9 K above the lowland of its 5° band and +7.2 K above the lowland within 500 km (63% of the cells have such lowland). Main: −2.7 K and +0.5 K. So the highland is not "too warm" against the free air, which is what the change intends. The comparison with the lowland looks large because the lowland itself sits 5 K below the sea of its latitude. **Change:** replace the Altiplano/Tibet paragraph (values from memory) with this. Open a follow-up to check whether tropical land 5 K colder than the sea of its latitude is plausible. On Earth the gap is small, and deserts are warmer than the sea.
2. **The shift also warms ordinary tropical uplands, not only highlands.** On identical topography (t = 0, highest cell 1.74 km, same in both sets) the branch warms land at 0–30° by +1.9…+4.2 K on average, and single cells by up to +9…+11 K (one seed +26 K, where main has land at 1.7 km and 33°S at −26 °C). By elevation at |lat| < 30°, at t = 0: 300–1000 m +2.4…2.5 K, 1–2 km +3.4…5.2 K. The cause is the ramp `w = z / 3 km` from sea level combined with finding 1's 5–9 K excess. Land at 500 m is inside the boundary layer, where the plan itself says the local balance holds. **Change:** start the ramp at the top of the boundary layer, e.g. `w = clamp((z − 1 km) / 2 km, 0, 1)`, or state and justify the current ramp in the PR.
3. **The side effect is plausible but not shown; `results.md` overstates it.** What I measured:
   - Highland precipitation rises with elevation and latitude held fixed. The rise is concentrated at 15–30°: 0.37→0.58 m/yr at 2–3 km (t +3.2) and 0.28→0.62 above 3 km (t +2.7). At 0–15° it is +20% (t ≈ 1). At 30–45° there is no change (0.42→0.42), although land there warms by 5–11 K. Highland evaporation doubles at 0–45° (recycling, as claimed). Lowland precipitation +5% (t ≤ 1.2).
   - "Erode faster" is not visible. Sediment to the ocean is *lower* in the branch: 3.82→3.40 km³/yr (t −1.6).
   - The lowering is not early, and it does not grow steadily. Per-snapshot mean land (main→branch): 100 Myr −4 m, 200 +20, 300–500 −50…−60, 600 +28, 700 0, 800–1000 −95…−145. Land %, flooded crust and crust > 50 km are flat or reversed at 500, 600 and 1000 Myr. All three planet-wide differences come from 700–900 Myr. Highland precipitation is already +0.1–0.2 m/yr from 100 Myr on.
   - Alternative: chance in collisions. In main, mean land follows collision stacking across seeds (r = 0.92; >50 km crust r = 0.80). The branch has less stacking at 600–1000 Myr (15.5→12.1 Gt/yr, t −1.4; at 300–600 it has more, 11.2→12.1). Regressing mean land on stacking plus a branch dummy leaves −23 m (t −0.6) for the branch. Crust > 50 km keeps −0.6 points (t −1.7).
   - The global +0.7 K and sea level do not explain it: the sediment part of sea level is lower in the branch, which would raise land.
   - Pairing by seed does not help at 500–1000 Myr. Topography is identical at t = 0, still correlated at 100 Myr (r 0.87–0.98), and decorrelated by 400–500 Myr (r −0.04…0.6). Welch is the right test there; paired tests are valid only up to ~200 Myr, where nothing differs.
   **Change:** say "possible, not significant". Drop "I take the effect as real" and the chain wording. Replace the seeds-0–3 table with 8 seeds; the 4-seed values exaggerate (next finding).

5. **Attribution: change 1 (ice-free start) has no effect in the runs.** `warmstart` s0 and s1 (with `free_air_height=0`) are byte-identical to `main` in every `cells_*.csv` up to 500 Myr. The main binary differs in size from final/warmstart, so it is the 7d26a11 build with the 15 °C start. This is expected: the initial topography is below ~1.8 km, so neither start glaciates anything in year 1, and the model keeps its state afterwards. Main's tropical ice (including −26 °C at 1.7 km, 33°S, at t = 0 in seed 6) forms from the local feedback, not from the start. **Change:** fill the attribution with this. Keep change 1 (harmless, fixes the unit-test case), but do not credit it with any run result. The −6.4 K abiotic offset of #29 is therefore not explained by the start either.
6. **Code: correct; minor points.**
   - Blend, band-centre interpolation (same as before, clamped) and AIR_SHARE are right.
   - `max(0, ·)` on the interpolated excess is consistent: excess is linear in the interpolated land, which the fine cell sees anyway.
   - Last year's excess pairs with last year's `previous_annual_land` in both the albedo loop and the output.
   - Only the year-1 albedo loop reads `ICE_FREE_START` before it is overwritten. The solar-sweep test resets `spun_up` but keeps the state, which is intended.
   - At `free_air_height = 0` the shift is exactly 0.0. Only the start differs from main, and finding 5 shows that it changes nothing.
   - Minor: the latitude fade uses the coarse cell's latitude (~4°), so between 30° and 45° the shift steps by up to ~25% at coarse-cell edges. Use the fine cell's latitude if it is cheap.
   - The shift is diagnostic. Highland evaporation doubles with no energy cost in the balance (acceptable, but say so).
7. **Unit tests.** The "off" case reproduces the runaway, which is meaningful. The "on" case is close to circular: at w = 1 the plateau equals the band sea by construction, and its ice follows from that. "Free ≥ local from an ice-free start" is a real but weak check. The polar `assert_eq` checks the fade. Missing: a 45° plateau (the fade zone), a plateau at 0.5–1.5 km (finding 2), and AIR_SHARE continuity.
8. **No cooling.** Tropical land above 3 km (reduced) is warmer in 8/8 seeds; so is land at 15–35° above 3.5 km. On identical topography (t = 0) the largest cooling of any land cell at < 45° is −1.5 K, at 840–980 m and 39°S in seed 6, through the coarse balance. In the branch, 11 of 4604 tropical > 3 km cell-snapshots are more than 3 K below their band's ocean.
